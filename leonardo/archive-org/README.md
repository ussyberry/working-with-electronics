# Leonardo da Vinci works from archive.org

Public-domain Leonardo da Vinci works — his notebooks, the treatise on painting, the codices and the drawings — plus public-domain books about him, downloaded from archive.org into this folder. Leonardo died in 1519, so his own works are all public domain. Filenames are kept exactly as they appear on archive.org.

Files larger than GitHub's 25 MB web-upload limit are marked 'archive.org only' below: they are stored locally, not in this folder on GitHub, and are available from archive.org through the links in the table.
