# Tesla works from archive.org

Public-domain Tesla works downloaded from archive.org into this folder.
All of these are Tesla's own writings, lectures, patents and correspondence,
or U.S. government records (FBI files) — public domain. Filenames are kept exactly as they appear on archive.org, except the four files from the 1956 Nikola Tesla Museum edition, whose leading spaces were stripped.

No in-copyright books are included here; where an archive.org item mixed
public-domain and in-copyright titles, only the public-domain titles were taken.

Five of the PDFs are larger than GitHub's 25 MB web-upload limit, so they are not stored in this folder. They are available from archive.org through the links in the table (marked 'archive.org only'): the Martin 1894 volume, the two complete-patents PDFs, the 1956 Museum edition, and the Tesla–Westinghouse correspondence.
